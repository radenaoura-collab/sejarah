Sejarah

Switzerland has a long history linked to the development of various regions in Europe. The territory of Switzerland was originally inhabited by various tribes, including the Celts, and later came under the rule of the Roman Empire. In 1291, three regions—Uri, Schwyz, and Unterwalden—formed an alliance to assist one another and defend their independence. This event is often considered a foundational moment for the Swiss Confederation. The name Schwyz also gave rise to the name "Swiss." Over the following centuries, more regions joined the confederation. Switzerland later underwent significant changes during the Napoleonic era. In 1798, the Swiss territory came under French influence and was reorganized into the Helvetic Republic.

After the Napoleonic era ended, the Congress of Vienna (1815) recognized and guaranteed Swiss neutrality. Subsequently, in 1848, Switzerland established a modern federal state with a central government and a federal constitution. Since then, the Swiss political system has continued to evolve into the federal state known today.

Geographical

The map above shows Switzerland, located in Central Europe. Switzerland consists of 26 cantons, indicated by different colors on the map. The country borders Germany, France, Italy, Austria, and Liechtenstein.

Switzerland features a landscape dominated by the Alps and is renowned for its lakes and natural scenery. Bern serves as the seat of government, while Zürich is one of the country's largest cities and a major economic hub.

Tour

Switzerland is a country renowned for its natural beauty and mountain tourism. Some of its famous tourist destinations include the Matterhorn in Zermatt, Jungfraujoch, Interlaken, Lake Lucerne, and the Rhine Falls.

Mattheron

The Matterhorn is one of Switzerland's most famous icons. The mountain stands 4,478 meters tall and has a distinctive peak shape. The Zermatt area is also renowned for hiking and winter sports.

Jungfraujoch

Jungfraujoch is a mountain area situated at an altitude of 3,454 meters and is known as the "Top of Europe." From here, visitors can enjoy views of the Alps and the Aletsch Glacier.

Lucerne

Lucerne is renowned for its views of the lake and the Alps. One of the city's icons is the Chapel Bridge, a historic wooden bridge that is a famous tourist attraction in Switzerland.

Rhine Falls

The Rhine Falls are located near Schaffhausen and are one of Switzerland's major natural attractions. The falls feature a massive volume of water and can be enjoyed from various viewing points or by taking a boat trip.

Interlaken

Interlaken is a destination surrounded by mountain scenery and situated between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. The town also serves as a starting point for various tourist activities in the Jungfrau region.

Chateau de Chillon

Château de Chillon is a historic castle located on the shores of Lake Geneva. It is one of Switzerland's famous historic tourist attractions and boasts a history spanning approximately 1,000 years.
